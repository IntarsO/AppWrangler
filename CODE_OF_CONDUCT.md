# Code of Conduct

AppWrangler follows the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.

In short: be welcoming, patient and respectful. Focus on what's best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/IntarsO/AppWrangler/security/advisories/new) (it's private, and fine for conduct reports too), or by direct message on GitHub to [@IntarsO](https://github.com/IntarsO). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour they consider inappropriate, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
